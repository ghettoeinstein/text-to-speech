Thank you for calling the Emergency Housing Hotline.

If this is a medical, mental health, or life-threatening emergency, please hang up and call 9 1 1 immediately.

This phone line is currently unavailable. Please do not call back and select other menu options in an attempt to reach another department or staff member. Other extensions are unable to assist with Emergency Housing matters. Repeated attempts to bypass the phone system may result in your phone number being blocked. We appreciate your cooperation.

If you are calling regarding JCOD Emergency Housing, please listen carefully to the following information.

Hours of Operation and Curfew:
The facility's hours of operation are 7 AM to 9 PM. Clients are expected to return to the facility no later than 9 PM. Any necessary accommodations for a late return must be discussed and approved before leaving the facility. If you arrive after 9 PM without prior approval, you will not be permitted to enter the facility. You may return at 7 AM the following morning.

Case Management or Facility Questions:
If you need to speak with your case manager or facility staff regarding your case, services, or other non-emergency concerns, please communicate with staff while you are at the facility. This hotline does not provide case management services.

Complaints and Grievances:
If you would like to file a complaint or grievance, please follow the Grievance Policy posted at the facility. Information regarding how to submit a grievance is available on-site.

Referrals and Intake:
Emergency Housing does not accept direct or walk-in intakes. Please do not arrive at the facility without an authorized referral. If you are seeking Emergency Housing services, you must contact JCOD and obtain a referral through the appropriate referral process, just as you did when you initially entered the program.

Please do not repeatedly call this number or select other menu options in an attempt to reach another extension. Those departments and phone lines cannot assist with Emergency Housing matters, and repeated attempts to bypass the phone system may result in your number being blocked.

Thank you for your patience, understanding, and cooperation.
